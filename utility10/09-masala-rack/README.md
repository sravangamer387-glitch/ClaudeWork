# 09 · Masala Rack (3-step spice rack)

**Tier A** · laser-cut · prototype Rs 850 · price Rs 1299. Status: designed and verified as far as a computer can; **no prototype built, nothing field-tested.**

## Problem
Spice jars crowd counters and the back row is hidden. A stepped rack puts 12 jars in view.

## How it works
Laser-cut 9 mm MDF: two stepped side panels, three shelves with end tabs that pass through the side slots, glue and wipe-clean finish.

## What I verified (and what I did not)
`docs/results_rack.md`: 4 jars of 62 mm fit a 290 mm shelf; a 6 mm shelf sags to span/215 under 4 full jars while 9 mm gives span/727, so 9 mm is chosen; all parts nest on one 600×450 sheet with no overlaps. Not verified: your jar size, MDF modulus, long-term creep.
