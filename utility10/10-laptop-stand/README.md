# 10 · Fold-flat Laptop Stand

**Tier A** · laser-cut · prototype Rs 700 · price Rs 799. Status: designed and verified as far as a computer can; **no prototype built, nothing field-tested.**

## Problem
Work-from-home laptops sit too low; a stand improves posture and airflow, but cheap ones wobble.

## How it works
Laser-cut 6 mm plate on a base with a notched strut for three angles (12°, 18°, 25°). Fold-flat for travel.

## What I verified (and what I did not)
`docs/results_stand.md`: stable (margin ×2.2–2.7, friction needed 0.24 vs 0.4 rubber) for a 6 N typing push with a 2.5 kg laptop; **a hard 15 N shove slides it and nearly tips at 25°** (honest limit); strut buckling factor 73; plate sag span/470. Nests on one 600×600 sheet.

## Manufacturing files
`../out/laptop_stand.svg` and `.dxf` (CUT layer = through cuts, ENGRAVE layer = marks). Generated by `../sims/sim_stand.py`; results in `../docs/results_stand.md`.
