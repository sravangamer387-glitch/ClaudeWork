# KINETIC economics (estimates, India, 2026; verify quotes)
Assumptions: 100 boards, JLCPCB-style assembly, StepStick modules bought separately (about Rs 150-600 each, not in the BOM).

| Item | Rs per controller |
|---|---|
| PCB 112 x 80 mm, 2 layer, assembled (about 55 parts) | 1,900 (estimate: assembly fee + parts) |
| ESP32-S3 module + sockets + connectors | 750 |
| Vibration puck (PCB + ADXL345) | 450 |
| Enclosure (3D printed) | 250 |
| **Total BOM + assembly** | **about 3,350** |
Sell price for makers: Rs 5,999 with puck, without driver modules. Margin about 40 % before shipping, support and certification. This is a guess, not a quote.

Prototype (1 board, 1 puck, 1 driver, 1 NEMA17 motor) is above the Rs 3,000 prototype ceiling once the motor and supply are included (about Rs 4,500); with a motor and supply you already own it is about Rs 2,800.
