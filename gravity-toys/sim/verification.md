## 3. Ramp racers (Toy 5)
Run time over 1.2 m (s). Sliding/rolling resistance coefficients are assumptions: hard wood 0.02, rubber tyre 0.04, felt 0.08. '-' = does not move.

| Angle | Rise mm | hard wood | rubber | felt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 deg | 105 | 1.91 | 2.27 | 5.73 |
| 10 deg | 208 | 1.26 | 1.35 | 1.61 |
| 15 deg | 311 | 1.01 | 1.05 | 1.16 |
| 20 deg | 410 | 0.87 | 0.90 | 0.96 |
| 25 deg | 507 | 0.78 | 0.80 | 0.84 |
- PASS: felt wheels are impractically slow at 5 degrees (> 3 s): use >= 10 degrees for felt (5.7 s)
- PASS: every wheel type moves at 10 degrees and the run is measurable (0.8-3 s) (wood 1.26s, rubber 1.35s, felt 1.61s)
- PASS: at 25 degrees fastest run is >= 0.5 s (phone stopwatch resolution) (0.78 s)
Mass independence: acceleration has no mass term when resistance is proportional to weight, so 20/40/60 g inserts should NOT change the time (that is the lesson). Real cars will differ slightly because axle friction is not purely proportional to load: expect a few percent.

## 4. Balancing bird (Toy 4): centre-of-gravity check
Masses: body 37 g, wings 13 g, weights 40 g (total 90 g). Weights hang 90 mm below the beak line.
**Result: the weights must sit 94 mm FORWARD of the beak tip** (wings swept forward) for the centre of gravity to be under the pivot (x_cog = 0.0 mm). The original design text did not say this; without it the bird tips backward.
Centre of gravity 43 mm below the beak tip (target >= 25 mm).
- PASS: CoG at least 25 mm below pivot (43 mm)
- PASS: horizontal CoG within 1 mm of the pivot
Rocking period about 1.00 s after a nudge (point-mass estimate).
- PASS: rocking period 0.6-2 s (lively but calm) (1.00 s)
If the weights are placed 5 mm further back than designed, the bird rests tilted about 3 degrees. Use adjustable weight screws so each bird can be trimmed.

### Roly-poly (self-righting)
- PASS: CoG height below the radius of the base (self-righting) (h=23.7 mm, R=35 mm)
Restoring lever arm at 90 degree tilt = (R - h) = 11.3 mm. Design target said 18 mm; with these masses the CoG is 23.7 mm: acceptable, but a heavier/lower slug gives a livelier return.
