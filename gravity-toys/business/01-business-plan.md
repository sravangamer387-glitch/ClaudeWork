Prepared 2026-10-31. Everything marked *(verify)* is from secondary sources or my assumptions. Money in INR.

## 1. Recommendation in one paragraph
Start with **two products in parallel** – **Timber Tower Marble Run** (best margin, hero product) and **Santulan Balance Set** (cheapest, craft story, good for gifting/export) – and run **Galton Board** as the third, school-channel product. Hold **Ramp Racers** and **Drop Quest** until the first two sell. Sell first on **Amazon.in**, then **own Shopify store**, **Flipkart**, and **Etsy (export)**. The one thing that can stop you is **BIS (ISI mark) certification**, so start it in week 1, not after the product is ready.

## 3. Tax and rules that decide how you sell
- **GST:** non-electronic toys moved from 12 % to 5 % from 22 Sep 2025; electronic toys 18 % *(verify against Notification 9/2025-Central Tax (Rate) or your CA – one source disagreed)*. Sources: [Tally – new GST rates](https://tallysolutions.com/gst/new-gst-rates-september-2025-business-impact/), [Busy – toys GST](https://busy.in/gst-rates/toys/).
- **BIS / ISI mark is mandatory** to sell toys in India (manufactured or imported). BIS has raided marketplace warehouses and seized uncertified toys; penalties up to 2 years / ₹2 lakh were quoted by a law firm *(verify)*. Sources: [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/bis-raids-amazon-flipkart-warehouses-for-selling-non-certified-products), [S.S. Rana](https://ssrana.in/articles/companies-consignment-seized-not-complying-bis/), [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1907200).
- **Micro-unit relief:** in 2023 the government said BIS would license micro-scale toy units without in-house test labs for one year and gave marking-fee concessions to micro units/startups/women entrepreneurs *(verify current status)*. 1,097 domestic toy units were licensed, 96.7 % MSMEs.
- **Handicraft/GI exemption:** one source claims Channapatna-type GI toys by registered artisans are exempt; I could not confirm this. **Do not rely on it**; ask BIS/your lab in writing.
- **Do not print "ISI", "BIS approved" or a licence number** on listings or packs until you actually hold the licence.
- **Legal Metrology** (packaged goods labelling: MRP, net quantity, month/year, manufacturer address, consumer-care contact) applies to retail packs *(verify with a CA or the Legal Metrology office)*.
- **Export (Etsy etc.):** BIS is for sale in India; selling to the US/EU needs ASTM F963 + CPSIA children's product certificate (US) or EN 71 + CE/UKCA (EU/UK). Etsy fees about 10 % all-in (listing $0.20, 6.5 % transaction, 3 % + $0.25 payments) before ads *(verify India payout rules)*. Source: [Etsy fee guide](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees).

## 4. The fastest legal route to market (decision for you)
| Route | Time to first sale | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. **Contract-manufacture at a vendor who already holds a BIS licence** that covers your product type, sell under your brand | 2–4 months | Lowest | Need to confirm whether the vendor's licence permits brand-owner marking (ask BIS / vendor in writing) |
| B. **Get your own BIS licence** for a factory you control or a vendor’s unit | 4–6 months | ₹2–5 lakh + effort | Most control, slowest |
| C. **Start with export/D2C-abroad** (Etsy, own site) while BIS is processed | 6–10 weeks | Test reports for ASTM/EN | Shipping cost and returns |
| D. **Sell non-toy "educational desk science kit" in India** | 1 month | — | Do not do this; calling it something else to dodge the toys order is a legal risk |

**My advice:** run **A + C together**: RFQ BIS-licensed makers now (Route A) and launch the Santulan set on Etsy/own site as soon as you have ASTM/EN test reports, while the India licence work continues.

## 8. Risks and mitigations
- **BIS delay** → start now; Route A/C; do not stock-up before clearance.
- **Cost overrun** → freeze design, fixed-price RFQs, 10 % scrap allowance.
- **Quality variance in handcrafted parts** → written tolerances + AQL inspection (file 02).
- **Copycats** → file trademark/design registration early; the puzzle (Drop Quest) is the most copyable.
- **Safety incident / recall** → age labels, lot codes, product-liability insurance quote.
- **Marketplace fees change** → rebuild the sheet each quarter.
