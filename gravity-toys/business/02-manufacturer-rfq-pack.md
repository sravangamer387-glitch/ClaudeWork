# 02 · Manufacturer RFQ Pack

Use this pack to get comparable quotes. Send the same RFQ to at least **3 vendors per process**. Vendor names are unverified leads from `../00-research-and-sourcing.md`.

## 1. RFQ email template
> Subject: RFQ – [Product] – pilot 200 pcs + 2,000 pcs/year – [Brand]
>
> Hello [Name],
> We are a new Hyderabad-based toy brand developing educational gravity toys. Please quote for the attached part list/drawing (Annex A).
> 1. Unit price at 200, 500 and 2,000 pcs (INR, ex-GST), lead time, tooling/setup cost, payment terms.
> 2. Sample price and sample lead time (3 samples).
> 3. Do you hold a valid **BIS licence (IS 9873) for this product category**? Licence number, scope, factory address. Can you mark products for a brand owner?
> 4. Materials and finishes you will use; test reports available for lacquer/paint heavy metals.
> 5. Photos of similar work; two customer references; factory certificates (ISO 9001, ethical audit).
> 6. Who owns tooling/moulds/jigs? We require it in writing that tooling paid for by us belongs to us.
> Please reply by [date]. We will visit factories for shortlisted vendors.

## 2. Vendor scorecard (score 1–5 each, weights in brackets)
Price (25) · Quality of sample (25) · BIS/compliance status (20) · Lead time & capacity (10) · Communication (10) · Financial/ethical standing (10).

## 3. Product spec annexes

### Annex A – Timber Tower Marble Run (starter set, 48 pieces)
- Wood: rubber wood, kiln-dried 8–10 % MC; no knots on running surfaces.
- Plates: 100×100×10 mm; post holes 20.3 mm square; groove 28 mm wide ×12 mm deep, R3 corners; tolerance ±0.3 mm.
- Posts: 20×20 mm square, 6 mm pin holes at 50 mm pitch; ±0.2 mm.
- Balls: Ø25 mm turned wood ×4 (Ø tolerance ±0.2); round, sanded, lacquered.
- Parts: 6 posts (240 mm), 8 plates, 6 ramps, 2 curves, 2 spirals, 1 funnel, 1 see-saw, 1 bell (lead-free brass), 1 tray, 20 cards.
- Finish: water-based or natural lacquer, 2 coats; grooves sanded + wax; migration of elements test report required.
- Edges R≥2 mm; no splinters; no pinch gaps.
- Packaging: kraft box with insert, English+Hindi manual, QR to video.
- Samples: 3 pcs; pilot 200.

### Annex B – Santulan Balance Set (bird + roly-poly + tumbler)
- Bird: body turned rubber wood, 160 mm long; wings 3 mm birch ply 220 mm span; 2 × 20 g lead-free weights captive; beak tip R1.5.
- Acceptance test: bird must balance on a 3 mm dimple perch for 60 s undisturbed; tilt 20° and recover.
- Roly-poly: Ø70 base, Ø60 body, 60 g steel slug sealed; must self-right from 90° tilt, 10/10 trials.
- Tumbler: R90 rocker, 25 mm tilt either side, returns to rest.
- Paint: water-based, lead-free, test report required; 3 colours per animal; artisan name card in box.
- Pilot 300 sets.

### Annex C – Galton Board
- Files: `cad/out/galton_back_mdf.svg`, `galton_front_acrylic.svg` (laser); acrylic 5 mm cast, MDF 6 mm low-formaldehyde.
- 55 pegs Ø4 mm stainless, 6 mm protrusion; hole Ø3.9 mm press-fit.
- Balls: Ø12 mm chrome steel ×30 (age 6+ warning).
- Back-gate slide cover; balls cannot escape in use.
- Acceptance: 100-ball run, no jams; centre bins collect most balls.
- Pilot 300 units (3 prototypes first).

### Annex D – Ramp Racers (hold until later)
- Ramp 1200×140 ply 12 mm; ladder stand 5 positions; 4 cars with 4 wheel types; steel weights 20/40/60 g captive.

## 4. Incoming QC checklist (per lot, AQL 2.5 major / 0 critical – adjust with your inspector)
1. Dimensions: 10 pieces/lot against drawing; fit-test stack.
2. Function: marble run end-to-end with 25 mm ball ×20 runs; bird balance test; Galton 100-ball run.
3. Safety: no loose parts that pull off with 70 N (confirm against standard); no sharp edges; coating adhesion; smell test; no exposed fasteners.
4. Finish: colour match, no sticky lacquer, no peeling.
5. Packaging: barcode/QR, labels (MRP, net qty, month/year, maker address, age warning, lot code).
6. Documents: test report, material certificate, invoice, packing list.

## 5. Payment and contract terms (suggested)
30 % advance, 70 % after approved pre-shipment QC; tooling ownership clause; confidentiality; right to audit; defect replacement; penalty for late delivery. Have a lawyer review.

## 6. Third-party inspection & testing contacts (leads, unverified)
Bharat Test House; Krishna Metallurgical Laboratories; Intertek Bengaluru. Ask each: fee list, sample count, turnaround, IS 9873 Part 1 & 3 coverage, whether they are BIS-recognised for the test scope.
