# Toy 5 · "Dhalaan" Ramp Racers Physics Lab

**One line:** An adjustable 1.2 m ramp, four wooden cars with swappable weights and wheels, and a
timing gate. Kids discover that heavier doesn't mean faster (ignoring friction) and that angle and
wheel type do.

| | |
|---|---|
| Age | 5+ (cars ≥ 45 mm wheelbase, no loose small wheels under 3) |
| Principle | Inclined plane, acceleration = g·sinθ − friction, mass independence |
| Price | ₹ 1,299 |
| Tooling risk | **Low–medium** – wood/ply; optional moulded wheels |

## 1. Plan
Wk 1–3 CAD + prototype ramp; wk 4–6 car/wheel friction tests; wk 7–10 pilot 300 kits; school lab
partnerships; later add a Bluetooth/IR timing gate (electric ⇒ IS 15644 scope).

## 2. Design
| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Ramp | 1200 × 140 mm, 12 mm birch ply, 2 lanes (60 mm each) with 6 mm side rails |
| Height | Ladder-stand: 5 positions → 5°, 10°, 15°, 20°, 25° (rise = 1200·sinθ → 105 / 208 / 311 / 410 / 507 mm) |
| Cars (4) | Body 90 × 45 × 30 mm, rubber-wood; 4 wheels Ø30 mm; steel axle Ø3 mm in brass bush |
| Wheel variants | Hard wood, rubber-tyred, felt-tyred, plastic – friction experiment |
| Weight slots | Cavity takes 20/40/60 g steel inserts, captive screw cover |
| Stop block | Cork-faced, adjustable |
| Timing | Two 3D-printed/laser gates with paper flag, kids use phone stopwatch; optional IR gate |
| Mission cards | 12 (e.g. "Does mass matter?", "Find θ for 1 s run") |

Physics check (simulation with assumed friction: hard wood 0.02, rubber 0.04, felt 0.08): over 1.2 m the run
takes 1.26 / 1.35 / 1.61 s at 10° and 0.78 / 0.80 / 0.84 s at 25°. **Felt wheels need ≥ 10°** (at 5° a felt car
takes about 5.7 s). Weights should not change the time, which is the lesson. Keep 10° default for visible
timing, 25° max for "launch" fun.

Steepness safety: ramp stand has 4 rubber feet and a lock-pin; cars stopped by cork block, not by kids' fingers.
