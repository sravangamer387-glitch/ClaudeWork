# Toy 1 · Timber Tower Marble Run ("Gurutva Tower")

**One line:** A modular, India-made wooden marble tower: stack plates on posts, route a ball down
ramps, spirals, see-saws and bells. Expansion packs drive repeat sales (the GraviTrax model, in wood).

## 2. Design
**Grid:** 100 × 100 mm plates, 10 mm thick, on 20 × 20 mm square posts. Posts have 6 mm pin holes at
50 mm pitch so plates clip at any level (stack height 50 mm increments).

| Piece | Spec |
|---|---|
| Ball | Ø25 mm turned wood (starter) · optional Ø25 steel-core "fast ball" age 6+ |
| Channel | 28 mm wide × 12 mm deep U-groove, routed, 3 mm corner radius |
| Straight ramp | 100 mm long, **8–12° fall** (Δh ≈ 14–21 mm) |
| Ideal solid-ball acceleration | a = (5/7)·g·sinθ → at 10° ≈ 1.2 m/s² (slow, watchable); at 15° ≈ 1.8 m/s² |
| Spiral | 1 turn, Ø100 mm centreline, 20 mm drop per quarter turn (≈ 10° slope) |
| Funnel | Ø90 mm → 28 mm exit, 35° wall |
| See-saw flipper | Pivot Ø4 mm dowel; counterweight so empty tilts to rest; ball mass 12 g |
| Bell/chime | Brass tube (lead-free) hanging in ball path |
| Catch tray | Cork-lined, 2 corner posts |

Starter set (48 pieces): 6 posts (240 mm), 8 plates, 6 straight ramps, 2 curves, 2 spirals, 1 funnel,
1 see-saw, 1 bell, 1 tray, 4 balls, 20 challenge cards. Expansion A "Loop & Lift", B "Split Path"
(diverter), C "Chime Orchestra".

Finish: water-based or natural lacquer, 2 coats; **inside grooves left sanded & wax-finished** for
consistent rolling (paint thickness changes friction).

Tolerances: groove width ±0.3 mm, post-hole pitch ±0.2 mm (laser/CNC); plates fit with 0.3 mm
clearance, test swell after humidity soak (Hyderabad monsoon 85 % RH).

## 6. Safety
Ball is Ø25 mm (< test-cylinder ~31.7 mm): label 4+ with small-parts caution; for 3+ variant use
Ø45 mm ball and wider groove (verify small-ball clause). Round all corners, no pinch gaps at posts,
lacquer must pass IS 9873 Part 3.
