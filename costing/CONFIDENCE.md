# Confidence ratings (ordering and works-as-intended)

Two columns for every project. They answer different questions:

- **Order confidence:** how sure we are that you can buy exactly these parts and files and get what the BOM says. **High**: every LCSC part live-checked (identity, package, stock above 500, not end-of-life) and the Gerber/BOM/CPL files pass all automatic gates. **Medium-High**: same, but one or more parts have limited stock (still enough for a prototype run) or a substituted part whose pad geometry is unproven. **Medium**: parts depend on a vendor module, a hand-sourced item or a manual cross-check. **Low**: no board or no verified parts yet.
- **Works-as-intended confidence:** how sure we are that the design does its job when built. An engineering judgement, **not a measurement**: nothing has been built. It weighs (a) how standard the circuit is (datasheet reference design versus novel), (b) the evidence we have (host-run firmware tests, simulation, pin-map checks, datasheet equations), (c) risks that only hardware can reveal (analog front ends, RF, power switching, mains, calibration, uncompiled firmware). **High** would need a built and tested unit, so no project is rated High. **Medium-High**: standard circuit, strong test evidence, low-risk failure modes. **Medium**: expect to find one or two issues in bring-up. **Low-Medium**: significant parts of the behaviour are assumed. **Low**: concept or software only.

**Plan for one respin.** Even a clean design review usually leaves one or two bring-up findings on a first board; order the small quantity first (5 boards) and keep the files in this repository as the single source of truth.
