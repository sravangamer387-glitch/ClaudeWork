Products built on one platform: **P1 Regional-language rhymes player**, **P3 Star projector lamp**, **P4 Handheld cricket game**.
Date: 2026-10-31. Status: **design and firmware complete to prototype level; nothing has been built or bench-tested.**

## Firmware design in brief
- One small HAL (`ap1_hal_t`): millis, buttons, UART, LEDs, 7-segment, buzzer. The same code runs on the MCU and in tests.
- **MP3 module protocol** implemented and tested against a worked checksum example. Command codes are from my recollection of the module datasheet: **verify them on the real module**.
- **Rhymes player:** language folders, track wrap-around, volume with a hardware-safety cap (20/30), sleep timer 15/30/60 min, survives clock wrap-around, restarts cleanly after sleep.
- **Star lamp:** warm white/blue/green/red/slow rainbow, 15/30/60 min timer with 10 s fade-out, lullaby loop. **Colour changes at most 1 level per 20 ms step** (no flashing – enforced by test, including a stalled main loop).
- **Cricket game:** 12-ball innings, timing zones (6 / 4 / 2 / 1 runs, early = out), missed ball may bowl you (seeded RNG, deterministic tests), speeds up every 2 balls, 4-digit display (runs, wickets, balls left).

5. **Content**: recorded rhymes/stories/lullabies with talent releases.
6. **Compliance**: IS 15644 / IS 9873 testing, BIS licence, labels, manuals.
7. Part numbers, prices and availability are unverified candidates.
