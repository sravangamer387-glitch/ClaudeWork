# Phase 5 · Wave 1 Design Briefs (Top 10)

Each brief is a starting specification for an engineer or EMS partner. Prices and BOMs are estimates from `top50.csv` (BOM at ~1,000 units; target price incl. GST). Safety first: no radio, no lithium, no cameras.

## 1. Regional-language rhymes & stories player (₹999, BOM ~₹330, age 2+)
Audio board + rugged rounded enclosure, 8 big buttons (play/pause, next/previous, volume, language, favourites, sleep timer), SD/flash with 100 rhymes + 30 stories per language; Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, English editions. 3×AA, screw-closed compartment, 0.5 W speaker. Differentiators: own recorded voice, bedtime timer, parent-lock volume cap. Packaging: window box.
## 2. Kids keyboard with Indian instrument voices (₹2,299, BOM ~₹780, age 4+)
37 keys, 100 tones including harmonium, tabla, sitar, flute, shehnai, 20 demo songs, record/playback, learn mode with key lights, mic input optional (no Bluetooth). Power: 4×AA or 5 V adapter (adapter must meet IS 15644 transformer-toy rules – consider battery-only in v1). Stand + songbook in Hindi/English.
## 3. Star projector night lamp with lullabies (₹799, BOM ~₹260, age 3+)
Rotating LED star/moon projector, 3 colours, 8 lullabies, 15/30/60 min auto-off, soft silicone shell. 3×AAA. Check heat and light-exposure limits; no flashing patterns.
## 4. Handheld electronic cricket game (₹699, BOM ~₹220, age 6+)
LED/segment display, bat-and-ball game with overs/runs, 2-player mode, sound effects, "IPL-style" scoreboard (no licensed names or logos). 3×AAA. Differentiator: India's biggest sport; clear rules card in Hindi/English.
## 5. Bilingual alphabet & number learning tablet (₹1,199, BOM ~₹420, age 3+)
Touch-button or key-based tablet, 6 learning modes (alphabet, numbers, words, quiz, music, draw-less), English + one regional language per SKU, parental volume limit. 3×AA.
## 6. Emergency-vehicle bump-and-go set (₹599, BOM ~₹200, age 3+)
Police, ambulance and fire engine with sirens and flashing LEDs; bump-and-go motor board; 3×AA; no small detachable parts; sturdy ABS. Cheap platform to learn motor-board manufacturing.
## 7. Doctor kit with light & sound stethoscope (₹699, BOM ~₹220, age 3+)
Role-play kit with a stethoscope that plays a heartbeat, a flashing torch (LED), thermometer prop and tools; electronics in sealed module; 2×AAA; plastic tools with rounded tips.
## 8. Snap-together electronics lab (₹1,299, BOM ~₹420, age 8+)
Snap modules (battery, switch, LED, buzzer, motor, light sensor, speaker) with 30 experiments, manual in English/Hindi, aligned to school science chapters. Low voltage ≤ 4.5 V; no soldering; modules sealed.
## 9. Tabla / dholak electronic pads (₹1,999, BOM ~₹650, age 4+)
Pressure pads with tabla/dholak/dhol/cymbal samples, 12 taals (Teental, Keherwa…), loop trainer, headphone out. 4×AA. Differentiator: cultural music learning; partner with a music teacher for taal lessons.
## 10. Dancing / crawling robot toy with lights (₹899, BOM ~₹280, age 3+)
Motor board, touch sensors, music and light show, random-walk behaviour, edge-safe design; 3×AA; covers over gears.

## Common deliverables for each brief (to be produced next)
Schematic and Gerbers; BOM with 2 sources per part; firmware spec; enclosure CAD; packaging dieline; manual; compliance checklist; RFQ; listing copy; challenge/lesson cards where relevant. These follow the same pattern as `gravity-toys/business/`.

## Recommended first three launches
1. Rhymes & stories player (content moat, highest score)
2. Star projector lamp (simplest, giftable, seasonal)
3. Cricket game (cheap, India-only appeal)
They share the audio board, so one platform investment covers all three.
