# Phase 2 · The Top 50 Electronic Toys (ranked)

**Method.** I brainstormed about 75 candidate electronic toys across 7 categories and kept 50. Each is scored 1–5 on Demand, Ease of build, Compliance ease, Differentiation and India fit (my judgement – not market data), plus a Margin index computed from the economics below.

Score = 0.30·Demand + 0.25·Margin index + 0.15·Ease + 0.10·Compliance ease + 0.10·Differentiation + 0.10·India fit.
Margin index: contribution % of net revenue ≥35 % → 5, ≥25 % → 4, ≥15 % → 3, ≥5 % → 2, else 1.

**Economics assumptions (all estimates – replace with quotes):** BOM at ~1,000 units (PCB, parts, plastic/enclosure, batteries holder); landed cost = BOM × 1.30 (assembly, pack, QC, scrap, freight-in); GST 18 % for electronic toys *(verify – 5 % applies to non-electric toys from 22 Sep 2025)*; referral 0 % under ₹1,000 and 8 % placeholder above; closing fee ₹30; shipping ₹80; ads 12 % of net revenue; returns 8 % of landed cost. Marketplace (Amazon.in) view; D2C/B2B margins are higher.

**Waves.** Wave 1 = rank 1–10 (build first), Wave 2 = rank 11–25, Wave 3 = rank 26–50.

## Notes on the ranking
1. **Indian-language audio and learning toys win** (ranks 1, 2, 5, 9, 11): regional-language rhymes/stories player, keyboard with Indian instrument voices, bilingual learning tablet, tabla pads, reader pen. They fit India-specific demand that imports do not serve well.
2. **Cheap impulse items rank mid-table but earn almost nothing on marketplaces** (glow spinner –₹2, solar kit ₹19, bristlebot ₹15, magic wand ₹15). Sell them only as add-ons, bundles or bulk to retailers.
3. **Radio features cost points**: 8 products use Bluetooth/2.4 GHz and need wireless approval work (compliance score 1–3). They are not in Wave 1.
4. **Marketplace contribution is thin (about 6–22 %)** at these prices and 1,000-unit costs. That is the central commercial problem and Phase 3 addresses it.
5. **Under-3 products** (lullaby soother 0+, rhymes player 2+) face stricter safety rules (battery compartments, small parts, sound pressure) – test before committing.

## Considered but excluded (examples)
AI chatbot "friend" robots and SIM-enabled kids smartwatches (privacy/child-data and telecom issues, high BOM); drones above nano class (registration rules); lithium power-bank style toys (battery certification burden); licensed-character electronics (royalties, IP); handheld game consoles with pirated game packs (IP risk); gun-type toys (restricted).
