# 10 · Katha Cubes

*Roll six story dice in English, Hindi or Telugu and tell a new story every time.*

**Ages 5+ · Players 1–6 · Printed + wooden cubes.** Status: designed, verified as far as a computer can; **no prototype built; nothing tested with children.**

## How it plays
1. Roll all six dice (Who, Where, Wants, Problem, Helper, Ending).
2. Tell a story using the six pictures/words; pass the dice to the next player.

## Design
Six 25 mm wooden or card cubes with printed face wraps, 36 faces in three languages. `sims/gen_story.py`.

## What I verified (and what I did not)
`docs/results_story.md`: 36 distinct words, 46,656 distinct stories, CSV for proofreading. Hindi and Telugu are unreviewed.
