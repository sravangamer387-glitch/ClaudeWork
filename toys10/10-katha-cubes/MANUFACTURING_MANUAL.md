# Katha Cubes: manufacturing manual

> **Status: files exist; Hindi/Telugu text is unreviewed; nothing printed.**

## 1. What is made

1. Roll all six dice (Who, Where, Wants, Problem, Helper, Ending).
2. Tell a story using the six pictures/words; pass the dice to the next player.

## 2. Files

- `toys10/out/katha_cubes_net.svg`: the face wrap net (36 faces = 6 cubes x 6 faces, 25 mm).
- `toys10/out/katha_cubes_words.csv`: the 36 words in English, Hindi and Telugu **for proofreading**.
- `toys10/docs/results_story.md`: 36 distinct words, 46,656 distinct stories.
- Icons: **an illustrator must draw the 36 pictures** (not done).

## 4. Making it

1. Native-speaker review of `katha_cubes_words.csv` (Hindi and Telugu) before printing.
2. Print the face wraps on vinyl sticker stock (matte), cut to 25 mm squares (crop marks in the SVG).
3. Sand the wooden cubes (Channapatna-style or MDF 25 mm), seal with a non-toxic water-based finish, apply one sticker per face, aligned to the edges.
4. Pack six cubes in a pouch with a rules card (3 languages).

## 5. Inspection

Six cubes, 36 distinct faces, stickers flat and aligned, edges rounded, no splinters.

## 6. Compliance and limits

Toys QCO / IS 9873 applicability and testing; age grade and small-parts warnings; Legal Metrology labelling; paint/ink heavy-metal report; no licensed names or logos.

Small parts: label 5+ with a choking warning for under 3. `docs/results_story.md`: 36 distinct words, 46,656 distinct stories, CSV for proofreading. Hindi and Telugu are unreviewed.

Needs an illustrator for icons; low price; small parts warning.
