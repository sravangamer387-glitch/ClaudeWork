# 08 · Rubber-band Racer

*Wind up the rubber band, release, race your racer across the floor.*

**Ages 6+ · Players 1+ · Laser-cut MDF/acrylic.** Status: designed, verified as far as a computer can; **no prototype built; nothing tested with children.**

## How it plays
1. Build the laser-cut chassis (no tools), wind the propeller-free rear axle with a thumb wheel, place on the floor and release.
2. Race on a 5 m track; try 2 or 3 bands, different wheels.

## Design
Two-band drive on a rear axle, chassis from 3 mm MDF, wheels from MDF discs with rubber O-ring tyres. `sims/sim_racer.py`.

## What I verified (and what I did not)
`docs/results_racer.md`: distance model. **My first constant was absurd (60 m); it was cut by 6×.** All results are estimates from assumed constants (5–12 m target) and must be measured with the first prototype.
