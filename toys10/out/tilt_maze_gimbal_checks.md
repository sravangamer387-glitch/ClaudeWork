# Tilt Maze gimbal: geometry checks

Sheet: 3 mm MDF, kerf 0.2 mm compensated. Dowel 6 mm: glued holes 6.2 mm, turning holes 6.6 mm.

Axles (6 mm wooden dowel, cut to length): A-left 40 mm (tray boss -> frame wall L -> knob), A-right 25 mm (tray boss -> frame wall R), B-front 45 mm (frame wall F -> upright F -> knob), B-back 25 mm (frame wall B -> upright B).
Assembly: glue the tray walls to the floor and the bosses inside the L/R walls; glue the frame walls at the finger joints (check square); push the uprights' tabs into the base slots and glue; fit axles; glue the knobs last.

- PASS: tray edge stays inside the frame height at +-8 deg (tray edge rises 14.4 mm; frame half-height 20 mm (+6 above))
- PASS: tray clears the frame walls sideways (gap 8 mm each side)
- PASS: frame clears the base at +-8 deg (lowest frame corner 24.1 mm above the base)
- PASS: plate fits the tray with 0.5 mm per side (tray inside 201 mm, plate 200 mm)
- PASS: each sheet fits a 900 x 600 mm laser bed (common CO2 9060) (tallest sheet 330 mm)
