Gearing note: a 1:2 axle step-up halves the turns needed but not the energy. A first version of this model used k = 0.0009 and predicted 60 m, which was physically absurd (a rubber band stores well under 1 J); the constant was cut to 0.00015 and the target is 5-12 m on tiles at 60 turns. If the first prototype gives under 3 m raise bands to 3 or reduce wheel friction. Everything here is an estimate from assumed constants, not measured.
